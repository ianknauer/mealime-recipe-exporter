Recipe Exporter for Mealime

Made by BellaNora, a Canadian meal planner.

Unofficial tool. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Mealime.

Saves your Mealime recipes (photos, ingredients, steps and nutrition) into a single zip on your computer. On my.mealime.com it also saves your current meal plan, the recipes you created yourself, and your recipe notes. Nothing is sent anywhere except Mealime.

You need to be on my.mealime.com or app.mealime.com and logged in, then click the extension icon from that tab.

  1. Open my.mealime.com (or app.mealime.com) and log in.
  2. From that tab, click the extension icon. This page opens next to it.
  3. Click Export my recipes. It takes about a minute for ~150 recipes.
  4. Unzip it and open index.html.
